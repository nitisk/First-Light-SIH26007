Open the project with KiCad 10. Keep the local symbol library and `sym-lib-table` beside it.

This is the wiring for the Uno/Pi tabletop build. Matching net labels mean the wires are connected, even where no line joins them on the page. Symbols show module header pins, not bare-chip pin numbers.

Power connections, component ratings and upload commands are in [hardware notes](../../docs/hardware.md).
