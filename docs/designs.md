# Design notes

- The full-size truck shows proposed LiDAR, camera, radar, GNSS and communication equipment.
- The demo electronics layout and circuit match the Uno/Pi tabletop controller.
- The electronics base is 250 x 205 mm. Parts shown around it are laid out for inspection.
- CAD dimensions are approximate component envelopes; mounting holes and fit need checking against purchased parts.
